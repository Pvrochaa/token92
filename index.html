        02 · Cycle

        A reactor cycle

        The cycle repeats endlessly. The more the community trades, the faster the core fills.

        1. 01

          A buy becomes fuel

          Every $T92 buy adds energy to the core, proportional to its value in SOL.

        2. 02

          The rods light up

          Each percentage point lights up one of the 92 rods. Follow it live on the site.

        3. 03

          Fission at 92%

          With every rod lit, treasury tokens get burned and the hash is published.

          The core cools down

          The level resets to zero, the cycle counter goes up, and the next run begins.

        The discovery

        Nobody knows who built Reactor 92. It showed up on the blockchain one random night, with all 92 rods dark and a single warning on the panel: "fuel with care."

        The first to arrive noticed every transaction lit up a rod. It wasn't electricity. It was community energy.

        Operator log, day 1

        The operators

        Whoever holds $T92 becomes an operator. There's no shift lead, just a shared panel and one rule: the core can never stay cold for too long.

        The operators learned to read the reactor. Below 60%, calm. Above 85%, everyone watches the counter.

        Operator log, day 9

        The first Fission

        When rod 92 lit up, the panel went white for a second. Part of the supply simply stopped existing, burned forever, with the receipt public on the blockchain.

        Nobody celebrated a profit. They celebrated the event. Then they reset the core to do it again.

        The name on the panel

        On the 25th Fission, the panel didn't go white like it always does. This time, for a moment, a line of text appeared in the noise: a signature, never seen before, from whoever coded the core.

        From that day on, Reactor 92 stopped being just a mechanism. It became a mystery the community itself chose to keep open.

        06 · FAQ

        Frequently asked questions

        When does $T92 launch?

        The launch is announced first on @T0KEN92. The contract address appears on this site at the same moment, and this panel switches from pre-launch to live data on its own.

        Does Fission guarantee the price will go up?

        No. Fission reduces the circulating supply, but price depends purely on market supply and demand. $T92 is a memecoin and promises no returns.

        Where do the burned tokens come from?

        From a public treasury wallet, set aside at launch solely for burns. Anyone can check its balance.

        How do I know a burn actually happened?

        Every Fission publishes its transaction hash. Just paste the hash into Solscan or Solana Explorer and watch the tokens go to the burn address.

        Can the dev create more tokens?

        No. Mint authority is revoked at launch, so supply can only go down.
